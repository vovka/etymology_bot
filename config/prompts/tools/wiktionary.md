Read the etymology and descendants sections of an English Wiktionary entry. Wiktionary covers words of every language, so use it to follow a word back through its ancestors, to check cognates and words sharing a root, and to read reconstructed roots. Page titles drop Latin and Old English macrons ("salarium", not "salārium"). For a reconstruction, turn a template such as {{der|en|ine-pro|*seh₂l-}} into the title "Reconstruction:Proto-Indo-European/seh₂l-".
