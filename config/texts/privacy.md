<b>Privacy</b>

• Words you send are used to write the answer. Answers are cached for up to 30 days without any link to who asked.
• Paying users: the bot stores your Telegram user ID, your plan, its expiry date and Telegram's payment IDs, to provide the plan and to handle refunds. Free users are not stored, apart from a per-hour request counter that expires after an hour.
• Messages you send to /support or /paysupport are forwarded to the operator (@vovkashch) with your user ID and username.
• Nothing is shared with third parties apart from the AI model providers, which receive the word and the source texts but not who you are. On the Unlimited plan, web searches about the word also go to a web search provider (Tavily), likewise without who asked.
